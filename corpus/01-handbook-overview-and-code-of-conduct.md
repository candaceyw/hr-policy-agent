# Handbook Overview and Code of Conduct

## Purpose
This handbook provides the general framework for how Northwind Robotics, Inc. expects employees to work, communicate, and comply with company policies. It is intended to summarize the expectations that apply to workplace behavior, time away from work, remote arrangements, benefits eligibility, expense handling, security, and problem reporting. The handbook is a practical guide to our policies and procedures and should be read together with more specific policy documents and local law.

This handbook does not create a contractual promise of continued employment, guaranteed benefits, or specific job duties beyond those described in a written offer letter, employment agreement, or applicable local law. Northwind may update or revise policies from time to time to reflect business needs, legal requirements, and operational changes.

## Company Values
Northwind Robotics is guided by values of integrity, accountability, respect, and customer focus. We believe employees should:

- act with honesty and professionalism,
- treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect,
- communicate clearly and responsibly,
- protect confidential information,
- raise concerns early rather than allowing risk to grow,
- support a workplace that is safe, inclusive, and productive.

## Scope of the Handbook
This handbook applies to employees, temporary staff, interns, and contractors where their work or role falls within company policy and the terms of their engagement. In cases where a policy conflicts with local law, collective bargaining obligations, or a specific employment agreement, the applicable legal requirement or agreement will guide the outcome, subject to the company's overall policies and local legal compliance.

Managers and HR may use this handbook as a baseline but may also apply role-specific requirements, department procedures, or local legal requirements.

## Workplace Expectations
Employees are expected to follow company policies and behave in a manner consistent with the values above. Certain standards apply to all employees, including:

- attendance and punctuality in accordance with the employee's schedule and manager direction,
- use of company time, equipment, and resources for legitimate business purposes,
- protection of confidential or regulated data,
- respect for colleagues and customers,
- truthful reporting of time, expenses, and workplace issues,
- adherence to safety, security, and compliance procedures.

Employees should not assume that a policy is optional because it is not repeated in every tool or workflow. If a question or issue arises, employees should ask their manager, HR, or the designated support contact for clarification.

## Code of Conduct
Northwind prohibits discrimination, harassment, retaliation, abusive conduct, and other behaviors that undermine an inclusive and respectful workplace. Employees must not discriminate against others because of race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates a hostile or intimidating environment, interferes with work performance, or affects equal employment opportunities.

Examples of prohibited conduct include bullying, threatening remarks, demeaning comments, sexual harassment, intimidation, retaliation against whistleblowers or reporters, or misuse of authority in a way that targets another employee or applicant unfairly.

## Respectful Workplace Standards
Employees are expected to communicate professionally and to handle conflict in a productive way. This includes:

- speaking respectfully in meetings and one-on-one discussions,
- avoiding hostility, intimidation, or public humiliation,
- using appropriate judgment in digital communications,
- raising concerns through proper channels when a problem is not resolved informally.

Managers are expected to model respectful conduct and respond promptly and fairly to reports of misconduct or harassment.

## Reporting Concerns
Employees who experience or observe misconduct should report it promptly. Reports may be made to:

- a manager,
- HR,
- the designated ethics or concerns channel,
- the company grievance process if appropriate.

Employees may report concerns in confidence to the extent permitted by law and company process. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and respectfully. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## Investigations and Fair Process
Northwind will review concerns in a fair and objective manner. Investigations may involve interviews, document review, and consideration of relevant policies and local law. Employees participating in an investigation are expected to cooperate honestly and maintain confidentiality to the extent required by the process.

The company may take corrective action, if warranted, including coaching, discipline, removal of access, or other action consistent with the findings.

## Security and Information Use
Employees are required to protect company information and to use company systems for legitimate business needs. This includes respecting access controls, safeguarding login information, and following the company data security policy when working remotely or on personal devices.

Employees should not use company resources in a way that violates privacy, security, or legal obligations. If an employee is unsure whether a use is allowed, they should ask their manager or the IT/security team before proceeding.

## Time Away, Leave, and Attendance
Time away from work is governed by the PTO policy, holiday policy, leave policy, and other attendance procedures. Employees should not assume that time off is approved until approved through the appropriate process. Requests must consider staffing needs, manager approval, and policy requirements.

## Expense, Travel, and Benefits
Northwind expects employees to use company resources responsibly and to request reimbursement only for approved business expenses. Benefits eligibility depends on employment type, tenure, and plan rules. Managers and HR may provide additional guidance for role-specific questions.

## Remote Work and Flexible Work
Remote work and hybrid work are available in roles where the work can be carried out effectively outside the office. Remote work requests require manager approval and may be subject to local law, business continuity, and security requirements.

## Conclusion
This handbook is the framework that helps employees understand the company's standards and the rules that govern daily work. It is not intended to replace specific procedures, local legal obligations, or manager guidance. When policies are unclear, employees should ask for clarification early.

Northwind encourages employees to raise questions before they become problems. Clear communication, careful judgment, and respectful conduct are essential to preserving a productive and compliant workplace.
