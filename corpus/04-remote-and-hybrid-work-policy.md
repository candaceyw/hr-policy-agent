# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose
This policy describes when remote and hybrid work arrangements are available at Northwind Robotics, how they are approved, and what expectations apply when an employee works outside a company office. Northwind supports remote work where it is operationally effective, legally compliant, and consistent with business needs.

## Definitions
- **Onsite** — the employee works primarily from an assigned Northwind office.
- **Hybrid** — the employee splits time between an assigned office and an approved remote location. The default minimum for an office-assigned role is **two days per week onsite**, though a team may set a higher requirement.
- **Fully remote** — the employee has no regular onsite requirement and works from an approved remote location.

## Eligibility
An employee may be eligible for hybrid or fully remote work when the role can be performed effectively outside an office and the employee meets the company's standards for workspace, security, and availability. Roles often suitable for remote or hybrid work include software, research, design, analytics, marketing, planning, and operations-support positions. Roles that typically require onsite presence include hardware and lab work, manufacturing operations, facilities, and some customer-facing or event roles.

## Core Standards
Before an arrangement is approved, the employee generally must meet all of the following:

- performance is satisfactory and there is no active performance improvement plan or open disciplinary matter,
- the employee has a reliable, private workspace and home internet of at least **25 Mbps** download,
- the employee can keep the agreed working hours and be reachable during core collaboration hours (10:00–15:00 local),
- the employee uses only company-managed devices and follows the Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy, including mandatory multi-factor authentication,
- the remote location is one where the arrangement is legally and operationally supportable (see the Out-of-State and International Remote Work Policy).

## Approval Process
Hybrid arrangements are approved by the employee's manager. **Fully remote arrangements require Director-level approval** in addition to the manager. People Operations reviews any arrangement that involves a change of work state, a new hire's first 90 days, or an accommodation request.

An approved arrangement is documented in writing and records:

- the remote-work location (city and state or country),
- the onsite requirement, if any, and the expected weekly pattern,
- working hours and availability expectations,
- equipment and security provisions,
- the next review date.

Arrangements are **reviewed at least every 12 months** and may be adjusted or ended based on performance, business needs, or compliance.

## Schedule and Availability
Remote and hybrid employees keep their agreed working hours and are reachable during core collaboration hours. Managers may designate specific onsite days for hybrid staff and specific in-person dates (planning sessions, team onsites, customer events) that fully remote employees are expected to attend, with reasonable notice and travel arranged under the Travel Policy. Employees are responsible for responding to messages, attending meetings, and meeting deadlines regardless of location.

## Workspace Requirements
The remote workspace must be secure, reasonably private, and free from distractions that would prevent focused work or confidential conversations. Work must be performed from the approved location; an employee who wants to work regularly from a different location must update the arrangement first. Working from a coffee shop, airport, coworking space, or other public setting is allowed only for short periods and only with the security controls in the Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy active (company VPN, privacy screen, no confidential calls in earshot of others).

## Equipment and Stipends
The company provides a standard equipment kit (see the Onboarding and Equipment Provisioning Policy). Approved remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a **one-time $500 home-office setup stipend**. Fully remote employees also receive a **$50 monthly connectivity stipend**. Company data may not be stored on personal devices, personal cloud accounts, or unapproved storage.

## Out-of-State and International Work
Working from a US state other than the assigned work state for **more than 30 days**, or working from another country for any period beyond brief incidental work, requires review and approval under the Out-of-State and International Remote Work Policy before the work begins. A short domestic trip of 30 days or fewer requires only advance notice to the manager.

## Business Continuity and Adjustment
A remote or hybrid arrangement may be modified or suspended if staffing needs, a business continuity concern, a security issue, or a documented performance problem requires it. Managers should document the concern, decide whether it is isolated or recurring, and consider a temporary adjustment before ending an arrangement. People Operations is involved whenever the change could affect performance status, pay, or legal compliance.

## Ending or Losing a Remote Arrangement
Remote work may be discontinued if the employee no longer meets the core standards — for example, an unreliable workspace, missed availability, a security violation, or failure to disclose a location change. The manager documents the reason and gives the employee a reasonable transition period to return onsite unless a security or legal issue requires immediate action.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: maintain a compliant workspace and connection, keep agreed hours, disclose location changes in advance, and protect company data.
- Managers: set clear onsite and availability expectations, review arrangements annually, and document any changes.
- People Operations: review work-state changes, accommodations, and any arrangement that raises legal or payroll questions.

## Summary
Hybrid work assumes at least two onsite days a week; fully remote work needs Director approval and an annual review. Employees need a private workspace, 25 Mbps internet, company-managed devices with MFA, and availability during core hours (10:00–15:00 local). A one-time $500 setup stipend and, for fully remote staff, a $50 monthly connectivity stipend are available. Working from another state for more than 30 days or from another country requires prior approval.
