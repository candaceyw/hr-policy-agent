# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose
This policy explains which expenses Northwind Robotics reimburses and how employees must request reimbursement for business costs. The goal is a process that is fair, consistent, cost-conscious, and compliant with accounting standards.

## General Rule
The company reimburses reasonable and necessary business expenses that are directly connected to company work and supported by proper documentation. Employees must use good judgment and choose the lowest-cost reasonable option. The company does not reimburse personal spending, duplicate claims, or costs that are not clearly connected to company business.

## Approval Before Spending
Advance, written approval from the manager (or the designated Finance approver) is required for:

- any single purchase of **$500 or more**,
- any trip with an expected total cost of **$1,000 or more** (see the Travel Policy),
- any equipment purchase, software subscription, conference, sponsorship, or client-entertainment cost,
- any expense that is unusual for the employee's role.

Approval should be obtained before the purchase. If it was not, the request may still be reviewed but reimbursement is not guaranteed.

## Eligible Expenses and Limits
Commonly reimbursable expenses, within these limits:

| Category | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Airfare | Economy for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or more with manager approval. No business/first class without VP approval. |
| Lodging | Up to **$250/night** standard cities, **$350/night** designated high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, and others on the Finance list). |
| Meals while traveling | Per diem of **$75/day** standard, **$100/day** high-cost cities. Alcohol is not reimbursable. |
| Personal vehicle mileage | IRS standard mileage rate for the current year (**70¢/mile**), for business miles beyond the normal commute. |
| Ground transport | Standard taxi, rideshare, rail, parking, and tolls for business travel. |
| Office supplies | Routine supplies required for work tasks. |
| Equipment / software | Only when approved in advance under the rules above. |
| Client meals / entertainment | Only with advance approval and an itemized receipt listing attendees and business purpose. |

## Ineligible Expenses
Not reimbursable unless the company approves an exception in writing:

- personal or family travel, and the personal portion of a mixed trip,
- alcohol, personal entertainment, and social activities,
- traffic or parking fines, and personal-vehicle repairs,
- personal items unrelated to work (clothing, toiletries, personal electronics),
- home internet or phone beyond the stipends in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy,
- purchases made without required advance approval,
- duplicate claims and expenses already covered by a per diem,
- upgrades, premium seat selection, and luxury options not required for business.

## Documentation Requirements
Every reimbursement request must include:

- the date of the expense,
- the business purpose,
- the amount and currency,
- the vendor or merchant name,
- an **itemized receipt for any expense of $25 or more** (a card statement alone is not sufficient),
- manager or Finance approval where required,
- for foreign-currency expenses, the converted amount or the card conversion.

Missing, altered, or illegible documentation can lead to delay, partial reimbursement, or denial.

## Submission and Timing
Submit expense reports through the expense system within **30 days** of the expense date, or within 30 days of returning from a trip. Reports submitted more than 60 days late may be denied unless the employee provides a reasonable explanation. Approved reimbursements are paid with the **next regular payroll run** after approval.

## Cash Advances and Corporate Cards
Cash advances are issued only with written approval for a specific business purpose and must be reconciled with receipts within 30 days; unused funds are returned promptly. Employees issued a corporate card must use it only for approved business expenses and reconcile every charge; personal charges on a corporate card are a policy violation.

## Compliance and Corrective Action
The company may deny reimbursement or require repayment for expenses that are ineligible, unsupported, or unapproved. Intentional misrepresentation, inflated claims, duplicate submissions, or altered receipts are treated as a serious policy violation under the Code of Conduct and may lead to corrective action up to termination.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: get approval before spending where required, keep itemized receipts, and submit within 30 days.
- Managers: confirm the business purpose and that limits and approvals were followed before approving a report.
- Finance: maintain the high-cost city list and per diem and mileage rates, review reports for policy compliance, and process payment.

## Summary
Reimbursement is for reasonable, necessary, documented business expenses. Get written approval before any purchase of $500 or more or any trip of $1,000 or more. Keep an itemized receipt for anything $25 or more. Meal per diem is $75/day ($100 in high-cost cities), lodging is capped at $250/night ($350 high-cost), mileage is the IRS rate, and alcohol and personal costs are never reimbursable. Submit within 30 days; payment follows the next payroll run.
