# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose
This policy explains what expenses Northwind Robotics will reimburse and how employees must submit claims for business-related costs. The purpose is to keep reimbursement processes fair, consistent, and compliant with company policy and accounting standards.

## General Rule
The company reimburses only reasonable and necessary business expenses that are directly connected to company work. Expenses must be both legitimate and supported by proper documentation.

Employees are expected to use good judgment and may not submit reimbursement for personal spending, duplicate expenses, or costs that are not clearly connected to company business.

## Eligible Expenses
Examples of potentially eligible expenses include:

- airfare or rail travel approved for a business trip,
- hotel expenses for approved travel,
- meals during an approved business trip or client-related travel,
- local mileage for business use when the employee uses their personal vehicle,
- office supplies required for work tasks,
- equipment or other work-related purchases specifically approved in advance.

Travel and accommodation should be reasonable and consistent with the business purpose of the trip. Employees should choose the lowest-cost reasonable option unless a more expensive choice is required for business reasons or was approved in advance.

## Ineligible Expenses
The following expenses are generally not reimbursable unless approved by the company in writing:

- personal or family travel,
- entertainment or social activities,
- alcohol or non-business meals,
- personal purchase items unrelated to work,
- duplicate claims,
- purchases made without approval when approval is required,
- excessive or luxury expenses that are not necessary for the business purpose.

The company may deny reimbursement for expenses that appear generous, excessive, or unrelated to work.

## Approval Before Purchase
Employees should obtain approval before incurring significant or unusual expenses, particularly when the purchase involves travel, large equipment, sales events, or non-standard costs. Approval can be granted by the employee's manager, department lead, or the designated finance approver.

If approval is required, the employee should receive written or documented approval before completing the purchase. When approval is not obtained in advance, the reimbursement request may still be reviewed, but approval is not guaranteed.

## Documentation Requirements
Every reimbursement request should include:

- the date of the expense,
- the business purpose,
- the amount,
- the vendor or merchant name,
- itemized receipts or equivalent documentation,
- manager or department approval if required,
- any other support required by the finance team.

Employees should submit clear, readable documentation. Missing or altered information can lead to delay, partial reimbursement, or denial.

## Timeliness and Submission
Employees should submit expense reports promptly and generally within 30 days of the date of purchase or travel. Delays may make the reimbursement difficult to approve or may lead to denial if records are incomplete or the expenses are no longer in the review window.

Any unusual expenses or missing receipts should be flagged promptly so finance can decide whether additional documentation is needed.

## Cash Advances and Employee-Owned Equipment
Cash advances or other prepayments may be allowed only when approved in writing and used for the approved business purpose. Any unused funds must be reconciled promptly.

Employees using their own equipment or paying personal expenses for work should keep evidence of the connection to company work and submit the information with the expense report.

## Compliance and Corrective Action
The company may deny reimbursement or require repayment when an expense is not eligible, not supported, or not approved. Intentional misrepresentation, duplicate claims, or false documentation may be treated as a policy violation and may lead to corrective action.

Employees should not submit reimbursements for personal expenses or alter supporting documents in any way.

## Summary
Reimbursement is available for legitimate business expenses that are reasonable, necessary, and properly documented. The company expects employees to obtain approval where required and to submit clean, accurate claims promptly.
