# Benefits Guide

## Purpose
Northwind Robotics offers benefits to support employee health, financial wellbeing, and long-term stability. This guide summarizes the main benefit options available to eligible employees and the general enrollment process.

## Eligibility
Benefits eligibility depends on employment classification, schedule, hours worked, and length of service. Typically, full-time employees are eligible for the standard benefits package after meeting the waiting period defined by the company and the benefit plan provider. Part-time employees may be eligible for a limited set of benefits if they meet the applicable thresholds or are otherwise eligible under local law.

Contractors, interns, and some temporary workers are not generally eligible for the standard employee benefits package unless their agreement or applicable law states otherwise.

## Benefit Categories
Eligible employees may have access to the following benefits:

- medical insurance,
- dental and vision coverage,
- retirement plan participation,
- flexible spending account (FSA) options,
- employee assistance or wellness support,
- other voluntary benefits offered by the company.

The exact plan menu may vary by year and by employee classification. Employees should review their annual enrollment materials for full details.

## Enrollment and Effective Dates
Benefits elections are generally made during the annual open enrollment period or after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, birth, adoption, or a change in employment status. Elections are not final until confirmed in the benefits system and approved under the company's process.

Employees should review plan costs, coverage levels, and required documentation before selecting a plan. If an employee misses the enrollment deadline, coverage may be delayed unless the employee qualifies for a special enrollment event or legal exception.

## Plan Selection and Costs
Employees choose benefit options based on eligibility, household needs, and cost. The company may contribute to some plans or provide certain coverage levels based on employment type, while employees remain responsible for their share of premiums or deductibles.

Employees should review benefit summaries and plan documents carefully. If they have questions about premium amounts, deductibles, coverage limits, or plan exclusions, they should consult the benefits administrator or HR.

## Changes in Employment Status
A change in employment status may affect benefits eligibility and effective dates. Examples include:

- moving from full-time to part-time,
- going on approved leave,
- returning from leave,
- termination or separation,
- conversion from contractor or temporary status to regular employment.

Employees should contact HR promptly when their employment situation changes so that benefits are updated correctly.

## Life Events and Special Enrollment
Qualifying life events may allow employees to make benefit changes outside the standard enrollment period. Common examples include marriage, divorce, birth or adoption, loss of other coverage, or a change in dependent status.

Employees must provide the required evidence and submit changes within the deadlines described by the plan or HR.

## Questions and Support
Employees with questions about their plan selection, coverage eligibility, premiums, and enrollment deadlines should contact HR or the benefits administrator. The company may also provide summaries and decision support during open enrollment.

## Summary
Benefits are an important part of employee support and may vary by employment type, schedule, and qualification period. Employees should understand their eligibility, enroll on time, and ask HR for guidance when their status changes.
