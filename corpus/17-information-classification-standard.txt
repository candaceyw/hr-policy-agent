NORTHWIND ROBOTICS - INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION STANDARD

PURPOSE
This standard defines how Northwind Robotics classifies information and the
minimum handling rules for each level. It supports the Data Security and
Acceptable Use Policy and applies to information in any form - documents,
email and chat, databases, source code, recordings, and printed material -
and to every employee, contractor, and intern.

WHY CLASSIFICATION MATTERS
Not all information carries the same risk. Classifying information tells
everyone how carefully to store, share, and dispose of it, so the company can
protect customer trust, meet legal obligations, and avoid preventable
incidents. When in doubt, apply the higher level and ask the data owner.

CLASSIFICATION LEVELS
The company uses four levels, from lowest to highest sensitivity: PUBLIC,
INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, and RESTRICTED.

PUBLIC
Definition: information the company has approved for release to anyone, or
that is already lawfully public. Examples: published marketing material, the
public website, press releases, published job postings, open-source code the
company has released.
Handling: no restrictions on sharing. Only the Communications or Marketing
team, or an approved owner, may designate something Public or release it
externally.

INTERNAL
Definition: information intended for Northwind employees and authorized
contractors that would not cause serious harm if disclosed but is not meant
for the public. This is the default level for day-to-day work product that is
not more sensitive. Examples: internal announcements, most project plans,
team documentation, non-sensitive meeting notes, the internal org chart.
Handling: share within Northwind on a business-need basis. Do not post
publicly or send to people outside the company without owner approval. Store
in company-managed systems. May be processed on a company-managed device.

CONFIDENTIAL
Definition: information that could harm the company, a customer, an employee,
or a partner if disclosed or altered without authorization. Examples:
customer data and customer lists, employee personal and compensation data,
non-public financial information, contracts and pricing, unreleased product
designs and roadmaps, most source code, security documentation, investigation
records.
Handling: access is limited to people with a specific business need. Share
only through approved company systems with correct permissions; external
sharing requires the data owner's approval and an approved transfer method.
Process only on company-managed devices with encryption and multi-factor
authentication. Do not copy to personal storage, personal email, or
unapproved cloud or file-sharing tools. Encrypt when sending outside the
company network. Label the document or message "Confidential" where the tool
allows.

RESTRICTED
Definition: the most sensitive information, where disclosure or misuse could
cause severe harm, legal liability, or a reportable breach. Examples:
government-regulated data, large sets of customer personal data, payment card
data, authentication secrets and encryption keys, security vulnerability
details before remediation, merger and acquisition material, litigation-hold
content.
Handling: access is granted only to named individuals approved by the data
owner and is reviewed regularly. Use only designated systems; do not download
or extract without owner approval. Encryption in transit and at rest is
mandatory. No use on personal devices under any circumstances. No use from a
location or jurisdiction not approved by Security. All access may be logged.
Sharing outside the approved group - internal or external - requires written
owner and Security approval.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
Data owner: the team or leader accountable for a set of information. The owner
assigns the classification, approves access and external sharing, and reviews
access periodically.
Data custodian: the team that operates the system where the information lives
(often IT or Engineering). The custodian implements the required controls.
All employees: apply the correct level to information they create, follow the
handling rules, and report suspected mishandling.

LABELING
Apply a visible label ("Internal", "Confidential", "Restricted") on documents,
spreadsheets, decks, and email subject lines where the tool supports it.
Unlabeled information is treated as at least Internal. A collection of
Internal items can become Confidential in aggregate (for example, a full
export of a directory); classify the collection at the higher level.

STORAGE AND ACCESS
Keep information in approved company-managed repositories with access limited
by business need. Do not place Confidential or Restricted information in
personal cloud storage, personal email, personal devices, or unapproved
file-sharing or note-taking tools. Use group-based access rather than
sharing broadly or via public links. Review access when a project ends or a
person changes roles.

SHARING OUTSIDE NORTHWIND
External sharing of Internal information needs owner approval. External
sharing of Confidential information needs owner approval and an approved
secure transfer method, and usually a confidentiality agreement. External
sharing of Restricted information needs written owner and Security approval.
Never use a personal account to move company information.

RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION
Keep information only as long as there is a business or legal reason, then
dispose of it using an approved method: secure deletion for electronic files
and media wiped or destroyed by IT, and shredding for paper. Do not destroy
anything subject to a legal hold. Follow the records retention schedule
maintained by Legal for specific record types.

TRAVEL AND REMOTE WORK
Carry only the information and devices you need. Use the company VPN on
untrusted networks. Do not display or discuss Confidential or Restricted
information where others can see or overhear. Follow the Out-of-State and
International Remote Work Policy for access from a new location, and the
Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy for border crossings.

INCIDENTS
If classified information may have been exposed, sent to the wrong person,
lost, or accessed without authorization, report it to the security team
within one hour and to your manager. Do not try to recall or delete evidence
yourself beyond an immediate, obvious step such as unsharing a link.

RELATED POLICIES
Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy; Out-of-State and International Remote
Work Policy; Workplace Conduct and Grievance Procedure; Leave of Absence
Policy; Handbook Overview and Code of Conduct.

SUMMARY
Northwind classifies information as Public, Internal, Confidential, or
Restricted. Internal is the default. Confidential and Restricted data stay on
company-managed devices and approved systems, are shared only on a
business-need basis with owner approval, and are encrypted when they leave the
company network. Restricted data adds named-approver access, mandatory
encryption at rest and in transit, and a strict no-personal-device rule.
Label what you create, keep information only as long as needed, dispose of it
properly, and report a suspected exposure within one hour.
