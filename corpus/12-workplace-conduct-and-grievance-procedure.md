# Workplace Conduct and Grievance Procedure

## Purpose
This policy describes the expectation that employees work in a respectful, lawful, and professional environment and explains how concerns about workplace conduct are reported and addressed. The policy supports a workplace culture grounded in respect, accountability, and fair process.

## Expected Standards of Conduct
Employees are expected to treat coworkers, clients, vendors, customers, and managers with respect and professionalism. This includes complying with anti-discrimination, anti-harassment, and anti-retaliation standards and following company policies and local legal requirements.

Northwind does not tolerate:

- discriminatory comments or conduct,
- harassment or bullying,
- threats, intimidation, or retaliation,
- abusive behavior in the workplace or online,
- interfering with another employee's ability to work safely and respectfully,
- creating a hostile work environment.

Employees must also avoid conduct that undermines the company's values, safety, or business integrity.

## Manager Responsibilities
Managers are expected to model respectful leadership and to respond promptly to concerns raised by employees. Managers should not ignore reports involving discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or abusive conduct. Managers must also ensure a working environment free from intimidation and must take corrective steps when behavior violates policy.

When a concern involves a manager, the employee may report directly to HR, a designated ethics channel, or another appropriate escalation path.

## Reporting a Concern
Employees who experience misconduct, witness misconduct, or suspect a serious policy violation should report the issue promptly. Reports may be made to:

- a manager,
- HR,
- an ethics or reporting channel,
- a designated grievance contact.

Employees are encouraged to provide specific details, including dates, locations, names of involved parties, and any supporting information they may have. Reports do not need to be perfect to be reviewed seriously.

## Grievance Procedure
Northwind will review concerns through a fair and confidential process. After a report is received, the company may:

- interview relevant employees,
- review records or communications,
- consider whether the conduct violated policy,
- determine appropriate corrective actions,
- document the outcome as needed.

The company will act as promptly as reasonably possible while preserving fairness and confidentiality. The process may involve HR, legal, department leadership, or an external resource depending on the seriousness of the issue and the applicable legal requirements.

## Non-Retaliation
Retaliation against an employee for reporting a concern, participating in an investigation, or speaking up in good faith is prohibited. Retaliation may include adverse action, exclusion, negative performance judgments, threats, or other forms of punishment. The company may treat retaliation as a serious policy violation.

Employees who participate in a grievance process are expected to cooperate sincerely and to maintain confidentiality to the extent required by the process.

## Privacy and Confidentiality
Reports will be handled in a manner that respects privacy as much as possible while allowing the company to investigate fairly. Information will be shared only with people who need to know for the purpose of review, follow-up, or required action.

Employees should avoid discussing sensitive complaints widely or making public accusations before the issue is reviewed. Doing so may complicate the process or create unnecessary risk for the people involved.

## Resolution and Follow-up
The company may take corrective action based on the facts, including coaching, written warnings, reassignment, removal of access, formal disciplinary action, or additional training. The company may also consider whether policy changes or supervisor training are needed to reduce future risk.

Some matters may require escalation beyond local management if the issue involves repeated misconduct, serious allegations, or a manager's role in the conduct.

## Escalation Path
Sensitive workplace concerns, repeated misconduct, or allegations involving a manager may be escalated to HR, legal, or a designated escalation process. Employees should not feel discouraged from escalating serious issues if they believe the matter is not being addressed appropriately or fairly.

## Summary
Northwind values a respectful, safe, and accountable workplace. Employees are encouraged to raise concerns early and to participate in a fair process without fear of retaliation. The company will review complaints seriously and take appropriate action consistent with policy and law.
