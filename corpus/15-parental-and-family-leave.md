# Parental and Family Leave Policy

## Purpose
This policy describes Northwind Robotics' paid parental leave and how it coordinates with job-protected leave, short-term disability, PTO, and state family-leave programs. For medical leave for the employee's own condition and for family-care leave, see the Leave of Absence Policy.

## Paid Parental Leave
Northwind provides **12 weeks of company-paid parental leave at 100% of base pay** for a qualifying event:

- the birth of the employee's child,
- the placement of a child with the employee for adoption, or
- the placement of a child with the employee for foster care.

The benefit is the **same for all new parents** regardless of whether they are the birth parent or the non-birth parent, and regardless of gender or caregiver designation.

## Eligibility
- Regular **full-time** employees and regular **part-time** employees who have completed **at least 6 months of continuous service** by the date leave begins are eligible.
- Paid parental leave is per qualifying event. For a multiple birth or the simultaneous placement of more than one child, the entitlement is a single 12-week benefit, not one per child.
- If both parents work for Northwind, **each** is independently eligible for the full 12 weeks.

## When and How to Take It
- Leave must be **started within 12 months** of the birth or placement and **used within that 12-month window**.
- It may be taken as one continuous block, or — with manager agreement — in up to **three separate blocks** of at least two weeks each.
- The birth parent's pregnancy-related medical recovery is covered first by short-term disability (see below); paid parental leave then follows. The two run back to back, not at the same time.

## Notice and Documentation
Give your manager and People Operations **at least 30 days notice** when the event is foreseeable, or notice as soon as practical otherwise. People Operations provides the request forms. Required documentation is one of: a birth record or a provider's statement of the birth date, or the adoption or foster-placement paperwork. Documentation is due **within 15 days** of the request and is stored with restricted access under the Information Classification Standard.

## Interaction with Other Leave and Benefits
- **Job protection:** parental leave runs concurrently with the 12 weeks of job-protected leave in the Leave of Absence Policy for employees who qualify for it, and with any state family-leave protection. An employee who does not yet meet the 12-month/1,250-hour job-protection threshold still receives the company paid parental benefit, and the company holds the role open for the paid-leave period.
- **Short-term disability:** for the birth parent, the short-term disability benefit covers pregnancy-related recovery (typically 6 weeks after an uncomplicated delivery, 8 weeks after a cesarean) at the rate in the Leave of Absence Policy; the **7-day waiting period is waived** for childbirth. Paid parental leave at 100% of pay begins after the disability period ends.
- **State paid family leave:** where a state program pays a benefit, the employee applies for it and the company **tops up** so total pay equals 100% of base pay; the company benefit is not paid on top of a full state wage-replacement benefit.
- **PTO:** employees do not have to use PTO before or during paid parental leave. PTO continues to be available and does not accrue during any unpaid portion.
- **Health benefits and 401(k):** group health coverage continues on the same terms during paid parental leave, with the usual employee premium share. 401(k) contributions continue from paid leave pay.

## Return to Work
The employee confirms a return date with People Operations and the manager. The role, or an equivalent one, is held for the paid-leave period (and longer where job-protection law applies). New parents returning from leave may request a temporary ramp-up schedule or other reasonable adjustment; People Operations coordinates with the manager. Employees who are nursing are provided reasonable break time and a private, non-bathroom space, consistent with law.

## Adoption Assistance
In addition to leave, the company reimburses eligible adoption expenses (agency and legal fees, court costs) up to a published annual maximum per child, with documentation submitted through the expense system. Details are in the annual benefits materials.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: give 30 days notice when possible, submit documentation within 15 days, and confirm the return date and any schedule request.
- Managers: refer the request to People Operations, plan coverage, hold the role, and support a smooth return.
- People Operations: confirm eligibility, coordinate disability, state benefits, and top-up, track the leave, and manage return-to-work.

## Summary
Eligible employees with 6 months of service get 12 weeks of parental leave at full pay for a birth, adoption, or foster placement, the same for every new parent, taken within 12 months of the event and in up to three blocks. For the birth parent, short-term disability covers medical recovery first (no waiting period), then full-pay parental leave follows. State paid-family-leave benefits are topped up to 100% of pay, not stacked. Health coverage continues and the role is held for the paid-leave period.
