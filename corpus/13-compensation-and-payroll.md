# Compensation and Payroll Policy

## Purpose
This policy explains how Northwind Robotics pays employees: the pay schedule, timekeeping, overtime, pay changes, and common payroll processes. It works with the PTO and Vacation Policy, the Expense and Reimbursement Policy, and the Benefits Guide.

## Pay Schedule
Employees are paid **semi-monthly**, on the **15th and the last day of the month**. When a pay date falls on a weekend or company holiday, pay is deposited on the preceding business day. Pay is delivered by direct deposit; employees keep their banking details current in the payroll system.

Each pay statement shows gross pay, taxes and withholdings, benefit deductions, employer contributions where shown, and net pay. Employees should review each statement and report a suspected error to Payroll within the current pay period.

## Exempt and Non-Exempt Status
Every role is classified as **exempt** or **non-exempt** under applicable wage-and-hour law:

- **Exempt** employees are paid a fixed salary for all hours worked in a period and are not eligible for overtime.
- **Non-exempt** employees are paid for actual hours worked, must record their time accurately, and are eligible for overtime.

An employee's classification is set by the role, not by preference, and is shown as `exempt_status` in the HR system. Questions about classification go to People Operations.

## Timekeeping
Non-exempt employees record all time worked, including start and end times and meal breaks, in the timekeeping system. **Timesheets are due by 10:00 on the business day after the pay period closes.** A late or missing timesheet can delay pay to the following cycle. Falsifying time records — recording hours not worked, or not recording hours that were worked — is a serious violation of the Code of Conduct.

Exempt employees do not track hours for pay but do record PTO and leave.

## Overtime
Non-exempt employees earn overtime at **1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek**, and at any additional rate required by state or local law (for example, daily overtime or double-time where it applies). **Overtime must be approved by the manager in advance.** Working unapproved overtime is still paid, because the law requires it, but repeated unapproved overtime is an attendance and planning issue the manager will address. Paid time off, holidays, and other paid non-working hours do not count toward the 40-hour overtime threshold.

## On-Call and Shift Coverage
Where a non-exempt role includes on-call or scheduled holiday coverage, the manager defines the schedule in advance. Compensation for on-call time and for working a company holiday follows the Holidays and Company Closure Policy and applicable law.

## Pay Changes and the Merit Cycle
Base pay is set within a pay band for the role, based on scope, skills, experience, and location. Pay bands are reviewed annually. The company runs an **annual merit review**, with approved increases effective **April 1**. Off-cycle changes may occur for a promotion, a significant change in responsibilities, an internal transfer, or a market adjustment, and require the appropriate management and People Operations approval. A promotion or transfer letter states the new title, pay, and effective date.

## Bonuses and Incentives
Any bonus or incentive plan (for example, a role-specific performance bonus) is described in a separate plan document that states eligibility, targets, and payment timing. **Employee referral bonuses are $2,000**, paid in the payroll run after the referred new hire completes **90 days** of employment, provided the referrer is still employed and was not the hiring manager or a recruiter for the role.

## Expense Reimbursements Through Payroll
Approved expense reimbursements are paid with the **next regular payroll run after approval** and are shown separately from wages on the pay statement. See the Expense and Reimbursement Policy for limits, receipts, and deadlines.

## Deductions and Garnishments
Standard deductions include taxes, elected benefit premiums, 401(k) contributions, and FSA/HSA contributions. The company processes court-ordered garnishments and child-support orders as required by law and notifies the employee. Employees may not waive legally required deductions.

## Final Pay
On separation, the employee is paid all earned wages by the deadline required in their work state, and any legally required PTO payout is included (see the PTO and Vacation Policy). Outstanding corporate-card balances, unreturned equipment, or an approved negative PTO balance may be reconciled from final pay only where state law permits.

## Corrections
If an employee is underpaid, Payroll issues a correction promptly, off-cycle if the shortfall is significant. If an employee is overpaid, the company arranges repayment on a reasonable schedule, consistent with law.

## Responsibilities
- Employees: keep banking and tax details current, submit accurate timesheets by the deadline, get overtime approved in advance, and review pay statements.
- Managers: approve time and overtime, plan coverage, and initiate pay changes through the correct approval path.
- Payroll and People Operations: run pay accurately and on schedule, maintain classifications and pay bands, and resolve errors.

## Summary
Pay is semi-monthly on the 15th and last day of the month. Roles are exempt (salaried, no overtime) or non-exempt (hourly, overtime at 1.5× over 40 hours a week with advance approval). Non-exempt timesheets are due by 10:00 the next business day after the period closes. Merit increases take effect April 1. Referral bonuses are $2,000 after the new hire's 90th day. Approved reimbursements pay out with the next payroll run.
